Accessibility help

This page explains how to use the dashboard Hotel guests in Lisbon and the Algarve with the keyboard only or with a screen reader. The dashboard has two screens: the main screen (guests, cancellations and price per month) and the detail screen (the countries of the guests).

Moving around a page

Keys for the whole page
KeyAction
TabGo to the next link, control or chart. The first two stops are the links "Skip to the charts" and "Skip to the filters".
Shift + TabGo back to the previous one.
Enter / SpaceUse the focused link or button (for example Reset filters, Previous, Next, Table, Play, Night mode).

Each chart is a stop of its own: Tab enters a chart and Tab again leaves it. The "Table" button of each chart shows, in the place of the chart, a table with every value the chart draws; press it again to see the chart. The whole dashboard fits one 16:9 screen from 1536 × 864 pixels (a full HD screen at 125 %); in a smaller window the page scrolls a little.

Moving inside a chart

Keys inside a chart
KeyAction
→ or ↓Next point, bar or country.
← or ↑Previous point, bar or country.
Alt + ↓ / Alt + ↑Change series, keeping the position: the other hotel in the monthly charts and in the countries chart (dumbbell).
Home / EndFirst or last point of the series.
Enter / SpaceShow the box with the exact value again. On the detail screen, also select the country (press again to clear the selection).
EscHide the box with the exact value.

A blue and white ring shows the focused point. A screen reader announces the series, the position (for example "Month 4 of 26") and the exact value; the other series is dimmed while you move.

  • Main screen: moving to a month in any chart marks the same month in the three charts (a dashed line in the line charts, a thick outline in the cancellations heatmap) and writes its values in the "Selected month" box. With the mouse, point at a month. In the heatmap, each row is a hotel and a year; the colour and the number of dots in a cell grow with the share of bookings cancelled (classes of 10 points).
  • Detail screen: no country is selected at first. Press Enter on a dot, a bubble, a box or a country of the map to select that country in every chart and in the "Selected country" box; press it again, or use the "No country" button, to clear the selection. The selected country has a light green band behind its row, a bold name, bigger outlined dots, a green box with white stripes (), a green bubble with the trail of the months already shown, and a green outline on the map. With the mouse, click a dot, a bubble, a box or a country of the map (click it again to clear it). The bubbles show the top 10 countries that have at least 20 stays in every month; any other country with at least 20 stays can be chosen in the "Selected country" list, and it then appears at the top of the country rows, and as one more bubble if it has at least 20 stays in every month.

Filters and other controls

Keys for the filters and controls
ControlHow to use it
Hotel (both, City Hotel, Resort Hotel)Tab to the group, then ↑ / ↓ to choose. The KPIs and charts change at once.
Year of arrivalTab to the list, then ↑ / ↓. 2015 (July to December) and 2017 (January to August) are partial years.
Reset filtersEnter: both hotels and all years again.
Selected country list (detail screen)Enter, Space or ↓ opens the list; ↑ / ↓ or the first letters of a name move in it; Enter chooses and Escape closes it. It has no country, the top 10 countries and Other, and the other countries with at least 20 stays. A point (●) on the right marks the countries that are not shown in the bubble chart with the current filters: the bubble chart shows only countries with at least 20 stays in every month.
Previous / Next (detail screen)Enter: the country before or after in the order of the rows. They are disabled at the first and last country.
No country (detail screen)Enter: clears the selection, so no country is highlighted.
Order of the countries (detail screen)↑ / ↓: by number of guests or from A to Z; "Other" is always last.
Chart / Map (detail screen)← / →: shows the countries as a dumbbell chart or as a world map, in the same place.
Play / Pause (detail screen)Enter: the bubble chart moves through the months, one every 1.5 seconds, and stops at the last month. Press again to pause.
Month slider (detail screen)← / →: previous or next month; Home / End: first or last month. The month is written next to the slider.
Table (every chart)Enter: shows the data table in the place of the chart, or the chart again.
Night mode (header)Enter: dark background and lighter colours; the choice is remembered in this browser. Without a choice, the page follows the setting of your system.
Notes about the data (footer)Enter: opens the notes (source, duplicated rows, currency); Esc closes them.

The line "Showing" under the filters always says which hotel and years are shown, and a screen reader announces it when it changes. The link to the other screen keeps the same filters.

Shortcuts of this dashboard

This dashboard has no extra keyboard shortcuts.

Screen readers and browsers

The charts are drawn as SVG with text alternatives: a short description, a longer one with the main values, and the data table. The pages were checked with the keyboard in Chromium. They have not yet been tested with a screen reader such as NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver; we would welcome your comments.

The dashboard is made for a computer or a tablet; it also works zoomed to 200 %, where the charts are shown one under the other. The animation only moves when you press Play, and it can always be paused.
