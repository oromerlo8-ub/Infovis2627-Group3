# Part 7. Work Group and Reflections

Group 3

## Work organization

We tried to work together as much as possible, especially in brainstorming sessions, where we discussed the dataset, our persona's questions and the charts that could answer them. Each of us refined the texts individually, trying to keep them brief, at about 300 words, because shorter texts are simpler to read and communicate our decisions more efficiently. The use of AI was limited to a few tasks. First, the creation of the dashboard, since we did not have enough knowledge of HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Vega-Altair to build it on our own. Second, the formatting of the PDF files and the correction of typos. Finally, we used it to make sure that our work followed what the course teaches, from the decisions of each chart to making sure the course's teachings are respected.

## Personal reflections

Using more "different" charts, beyond line charts and histograms, let us express ideas that are more complex. The calendar heatmap shows the cancellations of every month and year at once, the dumbbell chart compares how much each market weighs at the two hotels, and the animated bubbles follow a market through the year. Even though our dataset was somewhat limited, with only two hotels and a little over two years of bookings, we were able to create charts that we are satisfied with. As mentioned before, the use of AI allowed us to focus on creativity and on how to use the data, rather than spending our time learning how to build simple charts. In this way, most of our effort went into deciding what each chart should show and why, instead of into the code that draws it.
