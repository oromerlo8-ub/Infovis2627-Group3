Notes about the data

Source: Antonio, N., de Almeida, A. and Nunes, L. (2019). Hotel booking demand datasets. Data in Brief, 22, 41–49. Two hotels in Portugal: a city hotel in Lisbon and a resort in the Algarve.

Data: arrivals from 1 July 2015 to 31 August 2017 (2015 and 2017 are partial years).

26.8 % of the rows repeat an earlier row and are kept, so counts and cancellation rates may be too high. Prices: the currency is not stated; euros are assumed. The country code CN is taken as Canada.
