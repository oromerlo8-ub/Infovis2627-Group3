Barcelona road accidents: the audience

PERSONA

Jaume Collboni

Mayor of Barcelona

Goal
Cut serious road injuries; decide where to act
Domain
Expert in city policy, not in road-safety data
Charts
Reads simple charts; no statistics background
Platform
Tablet, landscape, in meetings and on the move
Frequency
Weekly briefing + before council sessions
Pain points
Little time; fewer incidents can hide worse outcomes
Needs to know
Is it improving, where, when, and what to do

Scenario. Before a council debate on road safety, the mayor opens the dashboard on his tablet. In under a minute he sees that incidents are down 23% but seriously injured people are up 23%, which districts and time slots concentrate the problem, and what is reliable enough to announce.

Questions the dashboard must answer
